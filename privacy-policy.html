Ringkasan

Jadwal Shalat menghitung jadwal shalat, arah kiblat, dan kalender Hijriah langsung di perangkat Anda. Aplikasi ini tidak memiliki akun, tidak menampilkan iklan, tidak memakai layanan analitik atau pelacakan, dan tidak mengirim data pribadi Anda ke server mana pun, termasuk server pengembang.

Data yang digunakan

DataUntuk apaDisimpan / dikirim
Lokasi perkiraan (izin lokasi perkiraan) Memilih kabupaten/kota terdekat agar jadwal shalat dan arah kiblat sesuai. Anda juga bisa memilih kota secara manual tanpa izin lokasi. Hanya kota terpilih yang disimpan di perangkat. Koordinat tidak disimpan dan tidak dikirim ke mana pun.

Koneksi internet

Aplikasi hanya memakai internet ketika Anda mengunduh atau mendengarkan contoh adzan dari katalog suara adzan. File tersebut diambil langsung dari Internet Archive (archive.org) dan Wikimedia Commons (wikimedia.org). Seperti permintaan internet pada umumnya, layanan tersebut dapat menerima alamat IP perangkat Anda dan informasi teknis permintaan; hal ini diatur oleh kebijakan privasi masing-masing layanan (Internet Archive, Wikimedia). Aplikasi tidak mengirim data pribadi apa pun kepada mereka. Jadwal shalat, kiblat, kalender, dan adzan tetap berfungsi tanpa internet.

Izin Android

  • Lokasi perkiraan: memilih kota terdekat (opsional).
  • Notifikasi: menampilkan pemberitahuan waktu shalat.
  • Alarm tepat waktu: membunyikan adzan tepat saat waktu shalat.

Pengaturan optimasi baterai dan mulai otomatis hanya dibuka bila Anda memilihnya sendiri dari menu Pengaturan.

Summary

Jadwal Shalat calculates prayer times, the qibla direction, and the Hijri calendar entirely on your device. The app has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking, and it does not send your personal data to any server, including the developer's.

Data the app uses

DataPurposeStorage / sharing
Approximate location (approximate location permission) Choosing the nearest city or regency so prayer times and the qibla are correct. You can also pick a city manually without granting location access. Only the selected city is stored on the device. Coordinates are neither stored nor sent anywhere.
App settings City, adhan mode per prayer, minute adjustments, calculation method, language, chosen adhan sound, and similar preferences. Stored in the app's private storage on the device.
Audio files you choose Used as the adhan sound when you pick a file from your phone. A copy is kept in the app's private storage and is never uploaded. The app only reads files you select yourself.
Compass sensor Showing the qibla direction. Used only while the Qibla screen is open; not stored.

Internet connection

The app uses the internet only when you download or preview an adhan from the adhan sound catalog. These files are fetched directly from the Internet Archive (archive.org) and Wikimedia Commons (wikimedia.org). As with any web request, those services may receive your device's IP address and technical request information, governed by their own privacy policies (Internet Archive, Wikimedia). The app sends them no personal data. Prayer times, qibla, calendar, and adhan alarms all work offline.

Android permissions

  • Approximate location: choosing the nearest city (optional).
  • Notifications: showing prayer time notifications.
  • Exact alarms: playing the adhan exactly at prayer time.
  • Run at startup: re-scheduling adhan alarms after the phone restarts.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock: playing the adhan to the end even when the screen is off.
  • Internet: only for downloading and previewing adhan recordings.

Battery optimization and auto-start settings are opened only when you choose them yourself from the Settings menu.

System location services

To determine your approximate location, the app uses Android's location services on your device (for example Google Play services location, when available). Their processing is governed by your device's location settings and the operating system provider's privacy policy.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone, because the app does not collect it.

Deleting your data

All data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time via App info → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. Downloaded adhan recordings and audio files chosen from your phone can also be removed individually on the adhan sound screen.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
